# Psychic.ai Claude plugin privacy

Updated October 26, 2026. Publisher: Psychic.ai, operated by MagicHaus.

This notice covers this skills-only plugin, not every service available on the
Psychic.ai website.

## Conversations in Claude

The skill helps Claude interpret questions and user-supplied tarot cards.
Those questions can contain personal or sensitive information. Identifiers,
credentials, location and birth details are not required or requested. Claude
processes the conversation under the user's Claude account settings, terms and
privacy policies. The publisher cannot set Anthropic's retention period.

## What the publisher receives

This plugin has no MCP server, code, authentication, telemetry, storage or
automatic network operations. It does not send questions, cards or conversations
to Psychic.ai, access saved readings, or invoke paid features. The publisher
receives and retains no conversation data through this plugin. This is not a
claim that Claude itself stores nothing.

## Optional links

Public Psychic.ai links contain fixed `utm_source=claude`, `utm_medium=skill`,
and `utm_campaign=tarot-reflection` parameters. They contain no question text,
cards, personal identifiers or birth details. Opening a link is a separate,
user-selected visit governed by https://psychic.ai/privacy. Website hosting and
any services the user chooses there have their own data practices. Readings do
not transfer automatically.

At a user's request, Claude may check public information using its own browsing
tools. The plugin declares no extra service or hidden transmission mechanism.

## Audience and contact

The skill is intended for adults and is not designed for users under 18.
It provides symbolic reflection, not diagnosis, professional advice or factual
prediction. Support and privacy contact: https://psychic.ai/contact.
